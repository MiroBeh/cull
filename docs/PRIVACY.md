# Rummage Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Rummage helps you clean up your photo library by swiping through photos to
keep or delete them. It is built to work entirely on your iPhone.

## What Rummage accesses

- **Your photo library**, after you allow it. Rummage shows your photos to you
  and, when you choose, asks iOS to delete them. iOS shows its own confirmation
  before anything is deleted, and deleted photos stay in the Recently Deleted
  album for 30 days.

## What Rummage stores

Only on your iPhone, in the app's own settings storage:

- The identifiers of photos you chose to keep, so they are not shown again for
  90 days. These are iOS's internal photo IDs, not the photos themselves.
- Lifetime counters: how many photos you deleted and how much space that freed.
- Your preferences, such as whether haptic feedback is on.

Deleting the app removes all of this.

## What Rummage does not do

- It does not send your photos or any other data off your iPhone. The app makes
  no network connections.
- It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party code.
- It does not track you or share data with anyone.

## TestFlight

If you test Rummage through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any
feedback you choose to send with the developer. This is handled by Apple under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
