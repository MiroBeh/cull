Cull · Design A

One Button

Nothing to choose. No month list, no filters — the app picks the next 50 photos and asks one question at a time, so a session is short and has a visible end. Big labelled Delete/Keep buttons; swiping still works, and the card tilts and stamps as you drag. The quiet Share button under the photo covers the other reason people dig through old photos: you find one you had forgotten about and want to send it to someone — through the system share sheet, which is why there is no artboard for it here.
